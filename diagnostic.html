Find your starting point

Answer some math questions, and we'll suggest which course to start with: Pre-algebra, Algebra 1, Geometry or Algebra 2. Then we'll make you a study plan.

  • It stops as soon as we know where to start: at least 4 questions, and never more than 28.
  • There's no timer, so take as long as you need. You can stop and come back later in this browser.
  • Work on paper. Please don't use a calculator or look things up. The plan is only useful if it shows what you can do on your own.
  • If you haven't learned something yet, press I haven't learned this yet. That gives you the best plan. Guessing doesn't help.
  • We won't say right or not as you go. You'll see how you did at the end.

This isn't a grade. It doesn't predict an SAT, ACT or placement-test score. It only tells you where to begin in this course.

For parents: what this is.

The questions are made fresh for each try and checked in this browser. A fixed rule picks the course from how many questions in each course were answered right. It isn't a standardized or nationally normed test, and the rule hasn't been checked against real learners yet: if the suggestion doesn't match what you know of your learner, trust what you know. You're welcome to sit with your learner and read questions aloud, but please don't help with the math. A Geometry result here means coordinate geometry; the check has no proof questions yet.
