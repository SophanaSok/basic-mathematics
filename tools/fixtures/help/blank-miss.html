Fill in the exponents: $2^3 \cdot 2^4$ is $2$ to the power , and $(2^3)^4$ is $2$ to the power .

Answer: $2^7$ and $2^{12}$

Three $2$s times four $2$s make seven; four copies of three $2$s make twelve, $2^{12}$.

  • Multiplying powers of the same base adds the exponents.
  • A power of a power multiplies the exponents.
