A course in sixteen chapters

Mathematics, built from the ground up.

Everything here is argued for, not asserted. Why is a negative times a negative positive? Why can't you divide by zero? Why is the square root of 2 not a fraction? Start at the integers and finish at determinants, with nothing taken on faith along the way.

The course follows the syllabus of Serge Lang's Basic Mathematics — the same topics in the same order, because the order is the point: each chapter is built only from what came before it. The explanations, figures, and exercises here are written for this site. If you have the book, read them side by side.
