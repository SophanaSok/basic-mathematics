What the course covers

This page lists every section of the course and of its skill packs, grouped by the course each is taught in, with what the section teaches, the Common Core standard that describes its work, and the parts of the SAT, the ACT, the Accuplacer and the ALEKS placement test that test it. The course follows Serge Lang's Basic Mathematics and assumes a learner ready to start algebra; the skill packs add the pre-algebra skills the gate exams ask for that the book leaves out. It is generated from the course's own records, so it is as current as the course.

The course has 104 sections with exercises: 72 in its 17 chapters and 32 in its 8 skill packs. Of the 55 exam topics the page tracks, 53 are taught in two or more sections. Two SAT topics are taught in one section each: Inference from sample statistics and margin of error (pk-data#samples is the course's one section on samples and margins of error); Evaluating statistical claims (pk-data#samples is the course's one section on evaluating a study's claim).
